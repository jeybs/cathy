Support

Need help with CATHY? Find answers to common questions, restore your purchases, report an issue, or contact support.

Contact Support

If you are experiencing a problem with CATHY, you can contact us directly using your email app.

When reporting an issue, please include your device model, operating system version, CATHY app version, and a short description of what happened.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a receipt or bill?

Open CATHY and choose the scan option. Capture or select a clear image of your receipt, bill, invoice, parking receipt, or supported document. CATHY will process the document and extract available transaction information.

Why is some receipt information incorrect?

Receipt extraction is automated and may occasionally misread damaged, blurry, faded, unusual, or very long receipts. Review extracted information before saving it and correct any fields when necessary.

Can I manually add expenses or subscriptions?

Yes. CATHY supports manually managed financial information in addition to information extracted from supported documents.

Why did I get logged out on my other device?

CATHY may allow only one active device session per account for account security. Signing in on a new device can end the previous active session.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Make sure notifications are enabled for CATHY in your device settings. Notifications may be used for receipt-processing updates, subscription reminders, bill reminders, and important account activity.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased CATHY Pro and your subscription is not showing correctly, first make sure you are signed in using the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

On iPhone or iPad

  • Open CATHY.
  • Go to the CATHY Pro or subscription screen.
  • Select Restore Purchases if the option is available.
  • Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple ID used for the purchase.

On Android

  • Open CATHY while signed in to the Google account used for the purchase.
  • Open the CATHY Pro or subscription screen.
  • Allow the app to refresh your subscription status.

If your subscription is active in Apple App Store or Google Play but CATHY still does not recognize it, contact support and include the platform you purchased from.

Report an Issue

Please include as much useful information as possible, such as:

  • What you were trying to do
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Your device model
  • Your iOS or Android version
  • Your CATHY app version
  • Whether the issue happens every time
  • A screenshot, when appropriate

For privacy, avoid sending full payment card numbers, passwords, authentication codes, or other highly sensitive credentials.

Subscription Help

CATHY Pro subscriptions purchased through the app are managed by Apple App Store or Google Play.

You can manage, cancel, or review your subscription from the subscription settings of the platform account used to make the purchase.

Deleting the CATHY app does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription.

Privacy

To learn how CATHY handles receipt images, financial records, account information, device information, AI processing, and other data, please review our Privacy Policy.
