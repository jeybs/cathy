Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CATHY collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our personal finance, receipt scanning, bill tracking, and subscription management services.

Effective date: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

CATHY ("CATHY", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal finance application that helps users scan receipts and bills, extract purchase information, organize expenses, view spending analytics, manage subscriptions, and receive reminders and account notifications.

By using CATHY, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use a CATHY account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account identifier, authentication information, and account status.

Receipts, Bills, and Scanned Documents

When you use scanning features, CATHY may process images of receipts, invoices, bills, parking tickets, statements, or similar financial documents that you choose to upload.

Information extracted from these documents may include:

  • Merchant or vendor name
  • Transaction date and time
  • Purchased items and quantities
  • Prices, taxes, discounts, totals, and payment details shown on the document
  • Expense categories
  • Bill or subscription information
  • Other text visible on the document you submit

Financial Tracking Information

CATHY may store information you enter or generate through the app, including expense categories, spending history, budgets, bills, recurring payments, subscription details, reminders, currencies, and related personal finance records.

Device and Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device identifiers, operating system information, app version, push notification tokens, login session information, error logs, and diagnostic information.

Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a CATHY subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play, we may receive transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription status, renewal status, expiration information, and related purchase verification data.

We do not directly receive or store your full credit card number when purchases are processed through Apple or Google.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide receipt, bill, and document scanning features
  • Extract and organize transaction information
  • Automatically categorize expenses
  • Generate spending summaries and analytics
  • Track subscriptions and recurring payments
  • Schedule bill and subscription reminders
  • Verify subscription purchases and manage CATHY Pro access
  • Send service notifications, including scan-processing results and login alerts
  • Maintain account security and enforce device or session policies
  • Diagnose errors and improve application reliability
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Artificial Intelligence and OCR Processing

CATHY may use artificial intelligence and optical character recognition technologies to analyze documents that you intentionally submit for scanning.

Receipt or document images, or portions of their contents, may be transmitted securely to third-party processing providers such as OpenAI for the purpose of extracting and structuring information from those documents.

We use these services only as needed to provide CATHY's scanning and data extraction functionality, subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy practices.

CATHY is intended to help organize personal financial information. Automated extraction may occasionally be inaccurate, so users should review important financial information before relying on it.

5. Push Notifications

CATHY may use push notifications to provide important service updates, including:

  • Receipt or document processing completion
  • Processing failures
  • Subscription reminders
  • Bill reminders
  • Login or account security notifications
  • Important account or subscription status changes

Push notification services may be provided through platform services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification Service.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

6. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

CATHY may offer paid features through Apple App Store and Google Play.

Payments are processed by the applicable platform provider. CATHY may send transaction information to our backend services to validate purchases, determine subscription status, and enable or disable paid features.

Apple and Google process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

CATHY may use third-party service providers to operate certain features of the app. Depending on the services enabled, these providers may include:

  • OpenAI — document understanding and data extraction
  • Google Firebase — push notifications and related application services
  • Apple — App Store purchases and platform services
  • Google Play — Android purchases and platform services
  • Cloud infrastructure providers used to securely host CATHY services and databases

These providers may process information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf or according to their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include encrypted network connections, authenticated APIs, restricted database access, access controls, secure credentials management, and server-side purchase verification.

However, no system or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements.

Certain transaction, subscription, security, or audit records may need to be retained for a longer period when required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing.

You may also:

  • Disable push notifications through your device settings
  • Stop uploading documents at any time
  • Review or correct extracted receipt and expense information
  • Manage Apple or Google subscriptions through the applicable platform
  • Request account deletion where supported

11. Account and Device Security

CATHY may limit the number of active devices or sessions associated with an account for security purposes. Signing in on another device may invalidate a previous session.

We may use device identifiers, authentication tokens, and login records to provide this functionality and protect user accounts.

12. Children's Privacy

CATHY is not intended for children under the minimum age required to independently consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If we learn that such information has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. International Use

CATHY may be used in multiple countries. Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, we take reasonable steps to handle cross-border data processing in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to CATHY, applicable laws, service providers, or our privacy practices.

When we make material changes, we may update the effective date shown at the top of this page and provide additional notice when appropriate.

15. Contact

If you have questions, privacy requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official CATHY support channel or support page provided with the application.
